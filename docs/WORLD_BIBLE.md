# The World Bible - flying OVER a world

`docs/WORLD_PLAN.md` is the story and the systems. This is the *place*: what the Bhagavata Purana
says the world looks like, and how each piece lands in a top-down shooter where the pilot flies
**over land**, not through space.

## 0. The diagnosis

Today the open play-space is black: a nebula at 10% alpha, stars, and pale rock islands floating in
it. Nothing is *under* the ship. That is space. The text says the opposite: the seven lower lokas
(*bila-svarga*, "heavens in holes") are each **as wide as the earth**, with cities, gardens, lakes,
rivers and soil - they only lack a sun. So:

- the **open space is the ground**, lit by jewels instead of sunlight;
- the **rock masses are raised ground** (cliffs, mesas, ruin blocks) and cast shadows on it;
- the **ship is in the air**: it casts a shadow, flies over drifting mist, under a jewelled vault;
- the **stars are the vault**: the underside of the world above, studded with the hood-gems of the nagas.

## 2. What the Bhagavata says is true of ALL the lower lokas (5.24.8-15)

These become the **world laws** every loka obeys:

1. **They are "imitation heavens"** - richer than Svarga. Not caves of misery: places people love.
2. **Maya Danava built their cities**: walls, gates, assembly halls, temples, guest-houses, with jewelled
   houses for leaders. -> every loka has *planned architecture*, not rubble.
3. **Gardens better than heaven's**: heavy trees wrapped in creepers, clear lakes of lilies and lotuses,
   water-birds. -> ground cover is lush; water is clear and bright.
4. **No sunlight, so no day, no night, no fear of time.** The jewels on the serpents' hoods remove the darkness.
   -> there is no sun and no clock; the light is warm, local and *comes from things*.
5. **Herbal elixirs**: no disease, grey hair, wrinkles or fatigue. -> nobody looks ruined; everything is
   polished. (Hull repair, health drops.)
6. **Their one fear is Time, the Sudarshana disc** - its light even causes miscarriages among the daityas'
   wives. -> *the disc is the only thing that scares them*: a recurring omen (a slow turning ring of light
   crossing the vault), and the reason a ruler's gaze matters.
7. Narada (Vishnu Purana) returned and said Patala was lovelier than heaven; the sun there gives joy
   rather than heat and the moon light without cold. -> the light is gentle, never harsh.

## 4. The surface and the great shape (Bhagavata 5.16-5.21)

Used for Bhu Loka and as the *shape of the whole world*, which later lokas can echo:

- **Bhu-mandala is a lotus**; Jambudvipa is its whorl, 1,000,000 yojanas across, with **Sumeru** at the centre:
  solid gold, 84,000 yojanas high, fire-bright, with Brahma's golden city on top. Four lakes of milk, honey,
  sugarcane juice and pure water; four gardens (Nandana, Caitraratha, Vaibhrajaka, Sarvatobhadra).
- **Rivers of fruit**: the Aruna (juice of mountain-sized mangoes, perfuming the air), the Jambu (elephant-sized
  rose-apples whose juice leaves golden *jambunada* on its banks), five honey rivers, rivers of milk, curd, ghee.
- **Seven islands, seven oceans**, each twice the one before: Plaksha (ocean of sugarcane juice; a golden tree
  with a seven-flamed fire at its root), Shalmali (liquor; a vast tree where Garuda lives), Kusha (ghee; kusha
  grass like a gentle fire), Krauncha (milk), Shaka (curd; a fragrance over the whole island), Pushkara (sweet
  water; a hundred-million-petalled golden lotus; the Manasottara mountain the sun circles). Beyond: **Lokaloka**,
  the wall-mountain that divides the lit lands from the unlit, held by four elephants; between the lands and the
  wall lies a *golden ground like a mirror in which anything that falls is never seen again*.
- **The sun** (5.21): one wheel (the year, twelve spokes), seven horses named for metres, driven by Aruna; it
  circles Manasottara round Sumeru - dawn, noon, dusk and midnight sit at four named cities. Underground there
  is no sun: that is what Bhu Loka loses at the gate.

## 5. How this becomes a top-down world (the render plan)

Seen from above the pilot needs: **ground**, **raised ground**, **air**, **sky**.

| layer | what | status |
|---|---|---|
| **vault** (sky) | the far stars become *gem-lights*: warm, twinkling, in the loka's colour; slow, deepest parallax | next (recolour of `Backdrop`) |
| **ground** | procedural painted land, per-band biome (palette, water, groves, roads, mandala rings...), generated in chunks from the world seed | **built** (`GroundMap`, `GroundLayer`) |
| **raised ground** | the existing wall tiles, now with a soft **cast shadow** on the ground | **built** |
| **mist** | slow drifting jewel-mist between the ground and the ship, parallax 0.9 (the ground moves at 1.0 against it: that gap is the altitude) | **built** |
| **shadows** | the ship and every enemy cast a shadow on the ground | **built** |
| **light** | ambient per biome (not one black): sunlit surface, warm jewel-light below, a tint per loka | **built** (`Biome`) |
| **props** | trees, lamps, lotus pads, mirror-pools, ruins baked into the ground; later as real sprites with sway | baked props built; sprites next |
| **events** | Sudarshana omen, Garuda's shadow, the Hataki fire, the ruler's gaze reddening the light | planned |

Rules: the ground is **never busier than the bullets** (it is dim and low-contrast; hostile rounds are the
brightest things); colour obeys the loka; a shadow is always offset the same way (the light is *overhead and
slightly toward the top-left*, because the gems hang on the vault).

## 6. Later bands and ideas worth keeping

- **Naraka as an optional hard mode** (the text names 28; the environmental ones suit a shooter): *Vaitarani*
  (a river that must be crossed under fire), *Asipatravana* (a forest of sword-sharp leaves: walls that cut),
  *Kumbhipaka* (boiling-oil lakes: damage floor), *Kalasutra* (a copper plain heated from above and below:
  heat that builds), *Avici* (a mountain of falling stone), *Andhakupa* and *Avatanirodhana* (dark pits with
  poisonous smoke). Used as hazards, not as punishment narratives.
- **Garuda's shadow** (Mahatala): the nagas fear him; a huge shadow crosses the ground and they flee: free kills.
- **Sudarshana**: a slow disc of light across the vault; everything *asura* flinches (enemies falter); the only
  clock the lower worlds have.
- **The golden mirror ground** (5.20): an out-of-bounds floor where things that fall are never seen again -
  the edge of the map for a boss arena.
- **Shesha's hoods** as the final background: the ground *is* a coil; the universe on a hood like a mustard seed.

## 7. The first slice (this commit)

Bhu Loka + all six Atala bands get their own ground, ambient and mist; walls and hulls cast shadows. Later
lokas add one `Biome` row each (palette, ambient, features) and the same generator paints them.
